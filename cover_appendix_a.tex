\noindent{\footnotesize\color{gray}%
\textit{Lloyd Shapley} (1923--2016) --- Nobel Memorial Prize in Economics, 2012. Developer of cooperative game theory and the Shapley value, a method for fairly attributing the total outcome of a joint process to each contributing factor. The Shapley value is the unique allocation satisfying efficiency, symmetry, and additivity: it averages each factor's marginal contribution across all possible orderings of inclusion.}
