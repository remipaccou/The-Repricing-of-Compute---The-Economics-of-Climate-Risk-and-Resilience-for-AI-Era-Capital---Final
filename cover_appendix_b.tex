\noindent{\footnotesize\color{gray}%
\textit{Wassily Leontief} (1906--1999) --- Nobel Memorial Prize in Economics, 1973. Developer of input-output analysis, a mathematical framework for tracing how economic activity in one sector propagates through an entire economy via inter-industry purchases. His method represents the economy as a system of linear equations where each sector's output depends on inputs from all others --- revealing the hidden interdependencies that connect seemingly distant parts of the economic system.}
